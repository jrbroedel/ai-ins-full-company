Rate movement by vehicle class

Charged rate per $100 of agreed value is the rate-on-line equivalent for this book: gross written premium at 100% divided by the agreed value it insures. Sample size is printed above every bar. Medians are computed over the bindings revealed so far.

Monthly rate consistency

Solid line is a three-month trailing mean; dashed is the raw monthly median. Points are hollow where the month carries fewer than five bindings, and hollow months are excluded from the rolling calculation.

The rate decline is a steady grind across all twelve months rather than a step change at any single renewal date. That pattern is consistent with market-wide softening rather than with one large account renewing at a materially different price.

State accumulation

Bars are net catastrophe exposure to capacity; the overlaid line is that state's share of the book on the right axis. Bars turn amber above 4% of book and red above 8%.

State shares here sum to 100%, and that is a property of this book rather than a normalisation. Every policy garages one vehicle at one address, so no policy is exposed in two states at once and there is no double count to reveal. The largest single-state accumulation is CA at 5.7% of book — a well spread position across 50 states. This is the opposite of the concentration problem a property book usually has, and it is the one number here that would change most if the book grew through a regional producer.

Limitations carried forward.
