What this rests on

  • Premium, commission, fees and policy counts are the reported book: 7,655 bound policies from bdx_uw_MASTER_2025-08_to_2026-07.xlsx. Nothing is re-rated.
  • Earning is straight pro rata on days from the effective date. The bordereaux carries no expiry column, so a 12-month term is assumed — if the book is written on longer terms, every earned figure here is overstated.
  • The 56% loss ratio is an underwriter estimate, not reported experience. For reference, the priced loss ratio of 53.95% is derived from the rating matrix loadings (gross-up divisor 0.58 over the 1.075 risk margin) and applies because every risk was written exactly at technical premium. is what the book was rated to produce — the estimate sits 2.0 points above it.
  • No reinsurance is deducted from the profit commission premium — confirmed as not applicable to this binder.
  • Commission stack: capacity allows 25% in total, of which Torque retains 12.5% and the producing broker takes 12.5%, leaving 75% net to the ten syndicates. The profit statement deducts the whole 25% because that is what the syndicate pays away; the revenue trend below counts only Torque's 12.5%.
  • Costs are the plan, not actuals. Only the retail commission line has been replaced with reported figures. Headcount, salaries and parent allocations remain unvalidated assumptions.
  • 1,620 policies — 21% of the book — were written below the $5,000 minimum premium, and the surplus lines tax rate is blank on every row. Neither affects profit commission, but both block invoicing.
  • The bordereaux marks its own rows as synthetic demonstration data.
